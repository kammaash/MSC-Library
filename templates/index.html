QR Code Library

Every item in the Digital Library gets its own QR code. Print this sheet and post it on a notice board, in a textbook, or at a reading corner — students scan it with any phone camera and the item opens straight in their browser (they'll need to be signed in, and be on the school Wi-Fi/network).
